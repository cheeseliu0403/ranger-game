Three Days as a Park Ranger
A short interactive fiction game made with Twine for the Hoosier Conservation Jam 2026 (theme: PEAK SOIL).
Play it
Play in your browser: https://cheeseliu.itch.io/three-days-as-a-park-ranger
Or download Three-Days-as-a-Park-Ranger.html and open it in any web browser — no installation needed.
The story
You are a newly hired ranger intern at a wetland nature preserve. After a heavy summer rain, the river has turned green and the fish are dying. Over three days, investigate the pollution, make your choices, and find out whether you earn the job.
Three days, two choices a day, three endings
Real Indiana conservation science: the White River, Craddock Wetland, the 4R nutrient approach, and the Grand Kankakee Marsh
About 10 minutes to play — click the green buttons to choose
